Before you buy

How fast do I get my licence key? +

Keys are issued by hand, so it is not instant — usually within a few hours of your purchase, sent to your Gumroad email as a JSON file. If nothing has arrived after 24 hours, reply to your receipt and it will be sorted out. Check your spam folder first.

How do I activate it? +

Open the app, go to the PARAMÈTRES panel → Modifier… → Licence, paste the JSON, and click Activer. It verifies offline and stays active forever.

What do I actually need to run it? +

Windows 10 or 11 and about 4 GB of RAM. No installation — it is a single executable you double-click. ExifTool is recommended for reading and writing metadata inside your files, and Ollama is optional if you want AI enrichment. Both are free, and the app works without them.

Is this a subscription? +

No. One payment, and the licence keeps working — offline, with no renewal and no expiry date.

Can I try it before paying? +

Yes — and you should. The free build on GitHub is the same application, including 3 free export runs. Buy only once you have seen it work on your own photos.
